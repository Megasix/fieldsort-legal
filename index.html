FieldSort Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

FieldSort ("the app") is a field data capture tool for photographing tagged subjects, reading tags with on-device OCR, and organizing and exporting the resulting photos. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

FieldSort stores everything locally on your device. It does not have user accounts, does not send your photos or field data to any server, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not show ads. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you deliberately export or share yourself, and, when you open the map to pin a site, the requests for the map images (see "Map" below).

Camera

FieldSort requests access to your device's camera to let you photograph tags and subjects within the app. Photos you capture are saved to the app's private local storage on your device. They are never uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere by the app itself.

Session and site information

Site names, optional coordinates, notes, tag codes, and session details you enter are stored in a local database on your device. Site coordinates are optional: you can type them, take them from your device's position, or pin them on a map.

Location

FieldSort asks for permission to use your location only when you tap "Current location" or "Where I am" to place a site, and only while the app is in use. It never tracks your location in the background.

Once you have allowed it, FieldSort also notes where your device was when each photo was taken (the "shot position"), using the position your device already knows, for audit purposes. It is stored on your device with the photo and appears in the CSV manifest when you export; it is never sent anywhere by the app. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device's settings; FieldSort keeps working without it.

Map

The map used to pin a site shows images ("tiles") from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. To show them, your device requests the tiles for the area you look at, which gives those servers your IP address and that area, as with any website. No photos, tags, or other field data are sent. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. The map is only loaded when you open it.

Exporting your data

When you export a session, FieldSort writes photo files (and an optional CSV manifest) to a destination folder you choose on your own device or a connected storage location. This is an action you take explicitly; FieldSort does not automatically transmit exported data anywhere.

Subscriptions and payment

FieldSort offers an optional paid subscription for unlimited use, handled entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play's billing systems. FieldSort never sees or stores your payment details — that information is handled by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies. FieldSort only receives a signal indicating whether a subscription is active.

What FieldSort does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No location tracking in the background
  • No sharing of your data with third parties

Data retention and deletion

Your sessions, tags, and photos remain on your device until you delete them within the app, or until you uninstall FieldSort, which removes all of the app's local data along with it.

Children's privacy

FieldSort is a professional field-work tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
